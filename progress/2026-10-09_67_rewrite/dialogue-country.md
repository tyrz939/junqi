2. country_keeper.d. BEFORE: "Halfway between what, people ask, and point at the sign. I tell them it was halfway when it was painted." AFTER: "People point at the sign and ask halfway to where. My father painted it. He says it was halfway when he painted it, and I've never had a better answer off him."
3. carters_note. BEFORE: "...Gone up on foot to see what it is looking at. Back directly." AFTER: "...Have gone up on foot to see what he is looking at. Back directly. Key is on me."

AFTER: "He'll be in at ten, on the stairs. I'll hear him. His door will be locked when I go up after." / "It'll have been locked since June. I'll have the only key."
5. story_leckie:offer_2. BEFORE: "He went in the barn after his dinner and he hasn't come out." AFTER: "He has his dinner at twelve.
